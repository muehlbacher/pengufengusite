Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy applies to all apps published by One Ant Studio (“we”, “us”), including Pengu Fengu.

Short version

Our games do not collect, store or share any personal data. Game progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Data we collect

None. We do not use accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone.

Purchases

If a game offers in-app purchases, payment is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Crash reports

If you have opted in to sharing analytics with app developers in your iOS settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you.

Emailing us

If you contact support, we use your email address and message only to answer you, and delete them once the request is resolved, unless we must keep them by law.

Children

Our games are suitable for all ages. Since we collect no personal data, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict processing of your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with the Austrian Data Protection Authority (dsb.gv.at).
