FAQ: Pengu Fengu

How do I play?

Tap the screen to jump. Land on the platforms and keep going as far as you can.

My progress or high score is gone.

Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app also deletes its data. If you lost progress without deleting the app, please email us.

The game crashes or lags.

Make sure iOS and the game are up to date, then restart your iPhone. If it still happens, send us your device model and iOS version.

I made a purchase but didn't get it.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Try restarting the game first. Refunds can be requested at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Does the game collect my data?
